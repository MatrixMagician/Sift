## Settled design decisions (resolved at plan time — do not re-litigate)

These five questions were left open by research and pattern-mapping. Each is
settled here with its rationale; the executor implements the settled answer.

**S-1 — Module placement: extend `src/sift/pipeline/eustack.py` in place.**
Rejected: a sibling `eustack_saturation.py`. Three reasons. (a) `mcm.py` (976
lines) and `perfmon.py` (811 lines) each keep their whole domain — detection,
grouping and flags — in one file; `eustack.py` at 373 lines plus roughly 230 new
lines stays well inside that precedent, and a sibling module has no analog in
this codebase. (b) The shipped
`test_no_ownership_attributed_lock_language_in_shipped_surface` reads
`eustack.py`'s full source at runtime, so extending in place puts Phase 16's
additions under that D-05 guard with zero test changes; a sibling module needs a
third `Path(...)` read added by hand — a second edit site for a security-relevant
invariant, which is exactly the drift ADR 0013 and the "shared, not copied"
convention exist to prevent. (c) `_is_resolvable()` stays a same-module call
rather than a second cross-module private import.

**S-2 — `_grade()` reuse: import it as-is, do not promote it.**
Use `from sift.pipeline.mcm import _grade` with a
`# pyright: ignore[reportPrivateUsage]` marker and a comment, following the
exact convention already established at `eustack.py:36-41` for
`_condense_symbol`/`iter_frames`. Promoting `_grade` to a shared home would edit
`mcm.py`'s shipped, tested surface for zero behavioural gain, and D-08 requires
`mcm.py`'s existing tests stay green. Verified safe: `mcm.py` imports nothing
from `sift.pipeline`, so `eustack.py -> mcm.py` introduces no import cycle.

**S-3 — The flag record: mint ONE `SaturationFlag` used by all three flag
families; confirm PATTERNS.md's "call `_grade()` at construction" finding, and
overrule its "reuse `DiagnosticFlag` for the two ratio flags" half.**
D-08 AMENDED's binding constraint is *do not widen `DiagnosticFlag`'s locked
ratio contract* — it does not require reusing that record. Reasons for one
record: (a) Success Criterion 5 requires every flag to print its raw value
**beside the configured threshold**, and `DiagnosticFlag` carries no threshold
fields, so a renderer would have to re-read config to satisfy SC-5; (b) a mixed
`tuple[DiagnosticFlag | SaturationFlag, ...]` forces every downstream consumer
(Phase 17 render, Phase 18 facts) to type-narrow on a union for no benefit;
(c) one record is less code than two. `SaturationFlag` copies `PerfmonHazard`'s
field shape (generic `value`, `Literal` severity) but — unlike `PerfmonHazard`,
which grades structural conditions — it DOES call `_grade()` at construction,
because all three Phase 16 quantities grade against two cut-points.
`SaturationFlag` deliberately carries no `event_ids`: `SignatureGroup` has no
per-thread event-id concept, and resolving an aggregate figure back to a citable
event set is Phase 18's open design question (STATE.md Blockers). The omission is
a decision, not an oversight, and must be documented as such in the docstring.

**S-4 — Default thresholds (arithmetic against the reference capture, D-09).**

| Config key | warn | critical | Reference-capture value | Margin to warn |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `unclassified_thread_pct` | 5.0 | 15.0 | 52 / 3,902 = **1.33%** | 3.8x |
| `no_resolvable_frame_pct` | 5.0 | 15.0 | subset of those 52, so **<= 1.33%**; measured **0.0%** on the committed derivative | >= 3.8x |
| `lock_convergence_count` | 5.0 | 20.0 | `__lll_lock_wait` matches **zero** times, so **zero sites** | n/a — no flag emitted |

Every default leaves the healthy capture graded `info`, satisfying D-09 with
margin. The two ratio pairs are calibrated against a single real capture (one
data point) and the count pair has **no** calibration data at all — no real
hung-server capture exists. Both facts go in the `EustackThresholdsConfig`
docstring so a future reader does not mistake round numbers for evidence.

**S-5 — `no_resolvable_frame` denominator: share of ALL threads.**
Confirmed as research recommended, and already settled by CONTEXT.md D-07's
2026-07-25 amendment ("Both ratio flags key on the THREAD-weighted denominator").
Both ratio flags therefore divide by `EustackAnalysis.total_threads`.

**S-6 — Flags are emitted at every severity including `info`.**
`mcm.compute_flags` appends each flag it computes regardless of grade; a flag
suppressed at `info` would never print its healthy value, contradicting Success
Criterion 5. "The healthy reference capture raises zero flags" (D-09) therefore
means **zero flags graded `warn` or `critical`**. A ratio flag whose denominator
is zero (an analysis with no thread events) is not emitted at all, mirroring
`compute_flags`' guard-before-dividing discipline — a missing signal emits no
flag rather than a fabricated figure.
