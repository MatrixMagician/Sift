## Settled design decisions carried from 16-01

S-1 through S-6 are settled in `16-01-PLAN.md`; S-7 in `16-02-PLAN.md`. Two are
directly load-bearing here and are restated rather than reopened:

**S-5** — the `no_resolvable_frame_pct` denominator is ALL threads
(`EustackAnalysis.total_threads`), matching the unclassified-share flag. This was
left genuinely open by D-07's original wording and is settled by D-07's
2026-07-25 amendment ("Both ratio flags key on the THREAD-weighted
denominator"): share-of-total is the operationally meaningful figure ("X% of the
whole capture has no symbols at all") and consistency between the two ratio flags
means a reader never has to check which denominator a percentage used.

**S-6** — a ratio flag whose denominator is zero is not emitted at all, so an
analysis with no thread events yields an empty `flags` tuple rather than a
fabricated `0.0`.
